As of right now, we are primarily developing the model in sim.js and the visualisation in index.html. The other files are for generating the default cache of results, and for running the model on a server (e.g. for a web app). 

The py files in this folder were initial prototypes for the model, and are not currently being developed. They may be useful for reference, but they are not necessarily up to date with the current model in sim.js.
